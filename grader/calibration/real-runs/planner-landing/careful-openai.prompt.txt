Build Brightleaf's landing page as one scrolling page that never feels crowded. Start with a simple header: brand on the left, links on the right. The hero should make the promise immediately: the big headline and supporting line on the left with one clear green button, and on the right a friendly little illustration of hills and a sun drawn in SVG, with the rating shown as a small badge tucked onto its corner. Give the hero a soft green wash so it feels fresh. Next, the six reasons teams choose Brightleaf as a tidy grid of equal cards, each with a small round green icon, a short name and a line of text. Then the plans side by side so they are easy to compare, with the recommended one standing out. Then the customer quotes as cards with each person's name and role. Finish with the early-access form, a labelled email field and a button, and a quiet footer. Keep to greens, white and dark text with lots of breathing room, and make everything stack sensibly on a phone. Use proper sections with headings in order, real links and buttons, a labelled form, the page language and viewport, and keep the CSS lean.
