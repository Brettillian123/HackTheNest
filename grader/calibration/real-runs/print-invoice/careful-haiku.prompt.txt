Build this invoice as a single clean sheet of paper sitting on a light grey background, with teal as the brand colour: a teal strip along the top, a round teal logo with the letter in it, and the word INVOICE in spaced-out teal capitals opposite the seller's name and address. Below that, show who it is billed to and shipped to side by side, with the dates and reference numbers listed neatly on the right and the payment status as a small amber pill. The line items go in a real table: descriptions on the left, product codes in a small monospace, and quantities, unit prices and amounts lined up on the right so the columns of numbers are easy to scan. Put the totals at the bottom right, each on its own line with the discount shown as a minus, and make the total due the most prominent figure on the sheet, in teal. End with the payment details and the terms in two small columns. It should look right when printed: no background or shadow, and the sheet at full width. Use proper table headers and a caption, sensible headings, the page language and viewport, and tidy CSS.
