About us

We are four friends who started welding frames in a garage in 2019. We wanted bikes that fit the person riding them, not the other way round.

Today we build about two hundred bikes a year, and each one is made by a single builder from first cut to final polish.

The workshop

An old boat shed by the Huron River, with room for six frames at a time.

The team

Two welders, a painter and a mechanic, all of whom ride to work.
