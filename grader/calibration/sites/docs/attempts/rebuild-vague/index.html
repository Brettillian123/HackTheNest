Welcome to Lumen

Lumen is a static site generator that builds websites from Markdown. This documentation walks you through everything you need.

Quick start

npm i -g lumen-cli
lumen new site

Sections

  • Installation: get Lumen on your machine.
  • Configuring: tailor Lumen to your project.
  • Deploying: ship your site.
  • Reference: all options.
  • Release notes: recent changes.

Lumen is open source. Contact the team at team@lumen.example.
