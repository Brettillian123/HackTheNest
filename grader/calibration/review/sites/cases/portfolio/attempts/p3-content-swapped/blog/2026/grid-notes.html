Notes on grids

30 May 2026

A grid is a promise to the reader about where things will be. Three grids I keep reusing and why the twelve-column one is rarely right.

Most of the work is deciding what to leave out. A tool that asks for attention all day wears people down; one that waits until it is needed gets picked up again tomorrow.

When I start, I list every screen and ask who would miss it. Usually a third go, and the rest get simpler.
