Frequently asked questions

Is the venue step-free?

Yes. The Corn Exchange has a lift to every floor and an accessible toilet on each.

Will talks be recorded?

Yes, with captions. Ticket holders get the videos a week later.

Is there food?

Lunch and snacks are included. Tell us about dietary needs when you reserve.

Can I transfer my ticket?

Yes, up to the day before. Email us the new name.

Is there a quiet room?

Yes, on the first floor, all day.

Do you offer refunds?

Full refunds until 1 April 2027, then half until 1 May.
