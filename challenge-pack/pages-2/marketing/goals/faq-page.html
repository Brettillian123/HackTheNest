Billing

Eight questions about plans, invoices and payments.

When will I be charged?

Your card is charged on the same day each month as the day you upgraded. Label postage is charged separately, at the moment you buy each label.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?

Yes. Switch in Settings, then Billing. Moving to yearly takes effect at once and credits any unused days of your monthly plan.

Where do I find my invoices?

Every invoice is listed under Settings, then Billing, then Invoice history, and a PDF copy is emailed to your billing contact on the day it is issued.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and American Express, plus ACH bank transfer on yearly plans.

Do you offer refunds?

Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.

How do I add a tax ID to my invoices?

Enter it under Settings, then Billing, then Company details.

What happens if a payment fails?

We retry three times over seven days and email you before each attempt.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.
