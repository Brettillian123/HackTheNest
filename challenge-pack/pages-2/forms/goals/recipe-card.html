Lemon and Herb Roast Chicken

A whole chicken roasted over sliced lemons and garlic, with crisp golden skin and juices that make their own sauce.

  • Time1 hr 30 min
  • Serves4
  • DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, about 1.6 kg
  • 2 unwaxed lemons
  • 1 head of garlic
  • 4 sprigs of thyme
  • 2 sprigs of rosemary
  • 40 g soft butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 150 ml chicken stock
  • Sea salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (180°C fan). Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking.
  2. Slice one lemon and halve the garlic head across. Lay them in a roasting tin with half the herbs.
  3. Mix the butter with the zest of the second lemon, the leaves from the remaining thyme and a good pinch of salt.
  4. Loosen the skin over the breast and push the butter underneath. Put the zested lemon inside the bird.
  5. Sit the chicken on the lemons, rub with olive oil and season. Roast for 1 hr 15 min, basting halfway.
  6. Rest the chicken for 15 minutes. Add the stock to the tin, scrape up the juices and simmer for 3 minutes.

Cook's tip

The chicken is done when the juices run clear from the thickest part of the thigh, or a probe reads 75°C.
