# Project context

`HANDOFF.md` is a good place to start. It covers how the team has been working, the event and its tracks, and the project's purpose and current direction. More detail is in `RESEARCH.md` and `IDEAS.md`.

In short, the team values accurate information (unconfirmed things are marked, not guessed), short readable docs, and suggestions that are labeled optional. New ideas are usually discussed with the team before they're added to `IDEAS.md`.
