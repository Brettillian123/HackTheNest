How Greenleaf Grocers cut checkout drop-off by 38%

A regional grocery chain was losing one in two online baskets at checkout. In twelve weeks we rebuilt the flow around speed and fewer decisions.

  • 38%fewer abandoned checkouts
  • 2.4xfaster checkout page loads
  • $1.2Madded yearly online revenue

The challenge

Greenleaf runs 46 stores across Oregon and Washington. Online orders had doubled since 2024, but almost half of the shoppers who reached checkout left before paying. The checkout asked for an account, five pages of details and a delivery slot chosen last.

What we changed

We cut checkout to a single page, made guest checkout the default and moved the delivery slot to the start, so shoppers knew their time before they filled in anything else. Page weight fell from 3.1 MB to 1.2 MB.

For the first time, our online checkout feels as quick as our fastest till.

Dana Whitfield, Head of Digital, Greenleaf Grocers

The results

The new checkout launched on 1 April 2026. Completion rose in the first month and kept climbing as returning shoppers got used to it.
